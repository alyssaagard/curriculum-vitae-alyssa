Research Profile

Historian and defense policy researcher. Master of Public Policy candidate at Rutgers University, concentrating in political processes and institutions with a research focus on foreign affairs and defense policy. Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Agard Research Associates Inc., a nonpartisan 501(c)(3) research and policy analysis institute in New Jersey. Present research, which I pursue independently of that institute, attends to the problem of strategic stability in an age of intelligentized warfare: escalation dynamics under incomplete information, PLA counterspace doctrine and the vulnerability of space-based nuclear command and control, the logic of systems destruction warfare, the susceptibility of military artificial intelligence to poisoned training data, and the organizational and cognitive habits of those who decide. Methods proceed by doctrinal genealogy, structured focused comparison, and process-tracing, joined to quantitative and geospatial modeling and to purpose-built simulation software. Where the established instruments of the field will not answer a question, I build my own. This work rests upon a formation in history, anthropology, and archaeology, with an abiding regard for material culture and the longue durée, for strategy is a human artifact and yields its logic most readily to those who study it as such.

Publications

Peer-Reviewed Articles

Agard, Alyssa I. "Deterrence in Orbit: PLA Counterspace Doctrine, JADO, and the Vulnerability of Space-Based Nuclear Command and Control." Journal of Advanced Military Studies. Forthcoming, Fall 2026.

Examines whether Joint All-Domain Operations (JADO), a doctrinal modernization advancing on a track largely separate from the modernization of nuclear command, control, and communications (NC3), accounts for the nuclear escalation risks that PLA counterspace capabilities pose to dual-use, space-based NC3 infrastructure. Locates the danger in a structural collision between American dependence on space for information advantage and a Chinese theory of victory that treats information systems as the primary target, bridging the nuclear-conventional entanglement and JADO doctrinal literatures, which have developed in parallel. Introduces the space-nuclear firewall as an analytical concept naming not the structural condition of entanglement but the doctrinal assumption that entangled systems may be managed without differentiated treatment, and argues that this separation is operationally fictitious: systems destruction warfare targets the adversary's operational system as an integrated whole, indifferent to the nuclear character of what it contains, whilst American architecture places nuclear functions inside the conventional target set, so that undifferentiated attack produces unavoidable nuclear degradation and dual-use ambiguity becomes an escalation liability rather than a deterrent asset. Concludes that JADO, whose emphasis on cross-domain convergence deepens this entanglement whilst leaving the risk unmanaged, does not position United States forces for success where the space and nuclear domains intersect, and proposes a dedicated Space-Nuclear Integration Cell within the JADO planning architecture.

Professional Journal Articles

Agard, Alyssa I. "Teach the Machine to Lose." U.S. Naval Institute Proceedings. Forthcoming; anticipated in the November 2026 issue. Second Prize, 2026 Marine Corps Essay Contest.

Argues that Marine stand-in forces, operating under persistent Chinese observation inside the first island chain, should become the joint force's forward data adversaries, presenting engineered and deliberately inconsistent signatures in peacetime so that the targeting and decision models the People's Liberation Army trains on those observations learn false associations before any conflict begins. Grounds the concept in PLA writings that name data as the foundation of intelligentized warfare and warn that such data can be poisoned, and in clean-label poisoning research showing that engineered but correctly labeled samples, gathered by the adversary's own sensors, can enter its training data without intrusion. Distinguishes the approach from evasion and decoying, which deceive a finished model only at the moment of classification, and from proposals that treat data poisoning as covert action, framing it instead as open military deception below the threshold of armed conflict within existing Marine Corps and joint doctrine. Reconceives counter-reconnaissance as the shaping rather than the mere denial of adversary collection, outlines an implementation that writes signature engineering into the training and readiness standards of forward units and sustains the effort as a standing campaign, and contends that PLA model hardening, far from foreclosing the effect, exacts its own toll in time, confidence, and trust.

Agard, Alyssa I. "The Intelligentized Security Dilemma: Systems Destruction Warfare, Technological Entanglement, and the Erosion of Strategic Stability." 2026. Under peer review at Space and Defense. Preprint available.

Argues that the convergence of the PLA's Systems Destruction Warfare doctrine with artificial intelligence, quantum sensing, hypersonic weapons, and counterspace capabilities creates an intelligentized security dilemma characterized by compressed decision timelines, the entanglement of nuclear, conventional, and space architectures, and nonlinear escalation pathways that render Cold War stability models obsolete. Traces PLA doctrinal evolution from mechanization through informatization to intelligentization, evaluates the internal frictions constraining modernization, and proposes domain-specific governance mechanisms tailored to the compound dynamics identified.

The Primacy Premium: Conditional Forecasts of Defense and Commercial Markets Under Chinese Military Primacy2026

Python modeling pipeline; in development

A conditional forecasting pipeline, calibrated on seven decades of IMF and SIPRI data, modeling how global defense spending, arms transfers, and commercial risk pricing would reallocate under three distinct pathways to Chinese military primacy by 2035. Reports full probability distributions across thousands of simulations per pathway, parses and validates official datasets, estimates response parameters by HAC regression, and exports analysis-ready tables for Power BI and Tableau.

https://alyssaagard.github.io/primacy-premium-preview/

The Coordination Tax: Field Artillery Engagement Simulation2026

Python, Streamlit, and static client-side build

Models field artillery engagement effectiveness across four reference platforms (M777A2, M109A7 Paladin, M142 HIMARS, and 2S33 Msta-SM2) through a dimensionless framework composing Amdahl's Law with exponential target perishability decay, isolating the coordination tax: the procedural delay that fires clearance imposes regardless of network speed. Features platform-specific weapon behavior, twenty-two academic citations, seven empirical scenario presets drawn from RUSI commentary on the Russo-Ukrainian War, and live-updating visualizations including a tactical engagement diorama, phase-decomposition timeline, and perishability decay curve. Operates as a self-contained analytical instrument, with parameterized inputs that let users test how far faster sensor-to-shooter links can improve engagement outcomes when clearance delay holds constant.

https://alyssaagard.github.io/coordination-tax/

ATLAS: Advanced Threat-Level Analysis and Simulation2026

Browser-based crisis wargaming platform, React

A research instrument for testing escalation thresholds, signaling dynamics, and the structural sources of inadvertent escalation under incomplete information, modeled on the NSC simulation methodology used at the National Defense University, Naval War College, and RAND. Users navigate multi-turn crisis scenarios through the DIME framework while an algorithmic White Cell adjudicates outcomes, a Red Cell generates adversary responses, and an eight-level escalation ladder tracks the trajectory toward or away from conflict. Ships with Taiwan Strait and NATO Baltic flank scenarios, a custom scenario builder, randomized injects, force disposition tracking, and a comprehensive After Action Review. Fully standalone with no external dependencies.

https://alyssaagard.github.io/atlas-wargame/
